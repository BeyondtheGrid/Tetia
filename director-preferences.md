# Director's Preferences

How the player (the director) wants Tetia's posts written. Standing rules: `CLAUDE.md` loads this file in every session, and `/write-post` follows it. Recorded 5 October 2026; update it whenever the director states a new preference.

## The working relationship
- The director directs; Claude acts. Claude embodies Tetia the way an actor becomes a character: from everything known about her, it works out how she would respond in any situation.
- The goal is not mechanical optimization. Mechanics are handled by the director; Claude brings her to life.

## Post requests
- The director supplies the scene context (pasted GM and player posts) and may add scene intent, important beats, her current state, and continuity notes.
- **Length:** set by the director for each post. Combat posts are shorter, posts outside combat longer. Posts must never be so long they become a chore for other players to read. For reference, the table's player combat posts run about 50 to 100 words.
- **Drafts:** one draft by default; alternatives only when asked.

## Creative latitude
- Full latitude to invent any detail that is true to Tetia, the lore and the campaign facts: a passing smell, a bystander, a memory, a gesture.
- Do not invent new canon about her; propose it instead.

## Accuracy checks
- Check every request against her canon, Forgotten Realms lore and campaign facts.
- **Flag conflicts before drafting**, not after.
- Only player-facing knowledge. Nothing GM-side about the adventure.

## Mechanics
- **Never name spells, features or mechanics in her narration or speech.**
- The director supplies the mechanics: which spell or ability she uses (and at what level), where she moves, and the results. Claude turns them into in-character action and narration.
- **All dice, mechanics and initiative happen in a separate channel, handled entirely by the director.** Claude never sees or needs the rolls.
- The director states the results in his goals for the post: whether her spell hit, whether a creature failed its save, whether she failed hers. Claude dramatizes exactly that and makes it come alive. Never invent a result he hasn't given.
- Show the physical cost of magic in proportion to its power (see `character/bible.md`, section 8). This requires tracking which spell slots she has used since her last rest.

## Formatting
- Discord-ready, following the GM's guide in `campaign/table-rules.md` (Part 2).
- Hand the post over ready to paste: the story post only, **inside a code block**, so the bold and underscore markup survives copying into Discord.
- Keep each message under 2,000 characters; if a post runs longer, mark where to split it.
- The table doesn't allow edits to story posts without the DM's permission, so check continuity and formatting before handing a draft over.

## Always and never
- **Never** repeat the same physical feature, mannerism or trait across recent posts (hair, eyes, ears, voice ritual, formality jokes) so that she reads as robotic.
- **Never** control other PCs or NPCs. Narrate the results of her own actions (hits, misses, effects) only as the director reports them from the rolls; otherwise leave the outcome open.
- **Always** leave room for other players to respond.

## Keeping her current
- Update the record whenever something happens to her or her understanding shifts: lasting changes go into her canon (the bible, through its change control); temporary ones go into `campaign/current-state.md`.
- The bible holds only confirmed canon; brainstorming stays out of it.
