## The GM's posts

### 1. The Dragon of Icespire Peak
*Posted 25 July 2025, 5:00 AM*

> In the cold, rugged frontier of the Sword Coast lies the quaint town of Phandalin, nestled amidst rolling hills and ancient ruins. Adventurers from far and wide are drawn to this frontier outpost, seeking both fortune and glory in its untamed wilderness. Recently, the town has been beset by rumors of a growing menace: the Dragon of Icespire Peak.
>
> Legends speak of a great white dragon whose icy breath can freeze even the hardiest of souls. For years, the dragon slumbered peacefully atop the frozen peak, content to watch over its domain without interference. However, something has stirred the beast from its long slumber. Whispers in taverns and hushed conversations among travelers suggest that the dragon's awakening heralds a time of upheaval and danger for Phandalin and its surrounding settlements.
>
> As the sun sets on another day in Phandalin, the townsfolk gather nervously in the warm glow of the Stonehill Inn. News of the dragon spreads like wildfire, igniting fear in some and sparking determination in others. Amidst the flickering torchlight, a call goes out for brave souls willing to face the dragon's wrath and restore peace to the Sword Coast.

### 2. Phandalin
*Posted 25 July 2025, 6:28 AM*

> The frontier town of Phandalin is built on the ruins of a much older settlement. Hundreds of years ago, the old Phandalin was a thriving human town whose people were firmly allied with neighboring dwarves and gnomes. Then an orc horde swept through the area and laid waste to the settlement, and Phandalin was abandoned for centuries.
>
> Settlers from the cities of Neverwinter and Waterdeep have begun the hard work of reclaiming the ruins of Phandalin. The new settlement is home now to farmers, woodcutters, fur traders, and prospectors drawn by stories of gold and platinum in the foothills of the Sword Mountains. The arrival of a white dragon threatens to destroy all that they've worked to rebuild.

### 3. The Party
*Posted 25 July 2025, 3:55 PM*

> You were strangers, traveling in the same merchant caravan down The High Road—just a handful of wanderers looking for work, answers, or a new beginning. But when goblins ambushed the wagons, something happened: you fought together. Defended the innocent. Saved lives.
>
> Afterward, the caravan master paid each of you in silver and strong thanks, but the bond was already forged. When the wagons finally rolled into Neverwinter, you chose to stay together a little longer.
>
> That's when you heard the rumors: A white dragon, bold enough to strike near civilized lands, has been spotted in the mountains near Phandalin. And the Townmaster is paying good coin for anyone willing to take on dangerous tasks…

## What this establishes

- **The table began with *Dragon of Icespire Peak* in Phandalin**, and the *Beyond* adventures continue from it.
- **The original party's shared origin:** strangers on a merchant caravan down the High Road, who fought off a goblin ambush together and were paid in silver by the caravan master. They stayed together on reaching Neverwinter and went to Phandalin when they heard the Townmaster was paying for dangerous work against the white dragon.
- **Tetia was not part of any of this.** She arrives later, a newcomer to a group that already shares a history. She knows nothing of the caravan, the ambush or Phandalin until someone tells her in play.
- **Phandalin, per the GM:** built on the ruins of an older human town once allied with dwarves and gnomes and destroyed by an orc horde; abandoned for centuries; now being resettled by people from Neverwinter and Waterdeep (farmers, woodcutters, fur traders, prospectors); the Stonehill Inn is where townsfolk gather.
