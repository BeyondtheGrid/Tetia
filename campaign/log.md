# Campaign Log

**What this is:** the story so far, organized by arc and condensed from the GM's recaps (`campaign/gm-recaps.md`, which holds the exact text and wins on any detail). Below that is the **running log**, where new events are added once Tetia is in play.

**Knowledge rule:** everything before Tetia's arrival is the party's history, not hers. She knows it only if someone tells her, or if it is common talk in Leilon and she hears it in play.

Sessions are weeks of real-world posting (the GM began on 27 July 2025). Session numbers are given so details can be checked against the recaps.

## The story so far

### 1. The road to Phandalin and the goblin cave (sessions 1–5)
- The original party, strangers from a caravan on the High Road, was ambushed by goblins on the way to Phandalin. **Clover**, a lone traveler, arrived too late for the fight and joined them.
- From a ridge above Phandalin they saw the **white dragon** in the distance. After a stop at the **Stonehill Inn**, they went back to track the goblins.
- **Tessa**, a traveling bard, joined. They spared the goblin cave's lone guard in exchange for information (goblins, wolves, bugbears and a human), drew the goblins out in waves, cleared the cave, killed the gang leader and rescued **Ander**, the missing human.

### 2. Phandalin's troubles and the Cult of Talos in the wild (sessions 5–20)
- Back in Phandalin: rumors of the dragon and of rising orc activity. The Townmaster sent them to warn outlying folk.
- **Umbrage Hill:** they talked a manticore into leaving **Adabra**'s windmill; Adabra refused to abandon her home.
- **Dwarven Excavation:** cleared two ochre jellies for the dwarves there; fought a band of orcs trying to claim the ruins; freed a **dragonborn** the orcs had held prisoner.
- **Butterskull Ranch:** ambushed by orcs on the trail, then broke an orc siege of the ranch and saved **Big Al**. They held funerals for both the fallen farmhands and the slain orcs.
- **Conyberry and the Shrine of Savras:** Clover (in lion form) and **Zinqys** (in elk form) caught a fleeing orc, who revealed a larger orc force at the shrine. **Aggy** and Tessa drew them out; a long battle followed. **Rokhan** joined during the fight, and **Kai** joined the next day (session 12). **Isa** touched the shrine's altar and had a vision of a white dragon resting atop a fortress.
- The captured orc revealed that orcs were gathering at the **Circle of Thunder** to summon **Gorthok**. At Conyberry they met **Sal**, a gnome warlock with a mastiff, who joined (session 13).
- **Neverwinter Wood:** twig blights at a charred pine; then the **Woodland Manse**, where they fought stirges, an orc warband and vine blights, and killed an **Anchorite of Talos** mid-ritual. **Kai found a map** to the Circle of Thunder.
- **The Circle of Thunder:** orcs ambushed them at a gulley in a sudden storm. At the hilltop, Anchorites of Talos and dozens of twig blights completed their ritual and summoned a **Thunder Boar** infused with storm magic. Several heroes fell unconscious, but the boar was killed and the ritual broken: a serious blow to Talos's influence in the region.

### 3. Mine, fortress, logging camp and barrow (sessions 21–35)
- **Ghouls** ambushed their camp on the road back (one fled). In Phandalin, Townmaster **Harbin Wester** and **Toblen Stonehill** told them of trouble at the mine.
- **Mountain's Toe Mine:** escorting the new overseer **Don-Jon**, they found six orcs killed by a dragon. The mine had been claimed by a wererat clan under **Zeleen Varnaster**; by bargain, the wererats agreed to leave and return to the shrine at Conyberry before the next full moon.
- They saw the white dragon feeding in the distance.
- **Axeholm:** an ancient dwarven fortress overrun by ghouls. **Moira**, a young dwarf woman, joined (session 26), and **Belrick**, a human warrior who uses both blade and magic, joined in battle (session 27). They destroyed a **banshee**, found a hidden emergency cache (coin, gems, supplies and a magical weapon), burned the ghoul remains and secured Axeholm as a refuge for Phandalin. **Samvell**, a dwarf monk, joined (session 28).
- **Tibor Wester's Logging Camp:** escorting supplies, they killed a bandit ambush and its captain; at the camp, ankhegs had taken the loggers. Under the riverbank they destroyed an ankheg nest shared with troglodytes, and found an unconscious humanoid in dark travel clothes and arcane markings scratched into the floor. **Kieran** joined here (session 31), having tracked the burrowing creatures.
- Across the river they found geomantic ritual anchor stones and a sinkhole cavern where cultists were widening "some underground breach." Kieran shot the **geomancer**; Belrick's fireball killed the rest; they destroyed an **earth elemental**, dismantled the ritual circle and closed the breach.
- **Dragon Barrow:** grave robbers withdrew peacefully and warned them of the larger mound. Inside, a test of worthiness: traps, will-o'-wisps, and the sarcophagi of four adventurers who had failed. The party won the **Dragon Slayer Longsword**. Outside they met **Daz**, a female orc cleric, who spoke cautiously of the sword's importance. The white dragon flew overhead.

### 4. Cryovain (sessions 36–41)
- The dragon's attacks escalated. With Harbin Wester, the party began evacuating Phandalin to Axeholm and set out for **Icespire Peak**, passing a massive orc frozen solid, killed not for food.
- At **Icespire Hold** they formed an uneasy alliance with the **Stone-Cold Reavers**, mercenaries after the hoard. The Reavers said the dragon never stayed long and often flew **southeast**, "as if drawn by something unseen."
- On the rooftop they fought **Cryovain**, the white dragon, who killed **Syleen** of the Reavers at once. **The dragon was slain.** The Reavers then turned on the party and were driven off.
- Phandalin welcomed them as heroes. Word came of trouble in **Leilon**.

### 5. To Leilon: the Cult of Talos (sessions 41–51)
- On the road south, **Tate** (a grim, driven hunter "marked by blood and purpose") and **Zeftina** (a halfling sorcerer "touched by restless, storm-laced magic") joined (session 41).
- **The Wayside Inn:** they broke a siege by undead and wraiths (Kai and Samvell suffered life drain) and met innkeeper **Backes Dunfiel**, **Cooragh** of the legendary pork pie, the bard **Tarbin Tul** (full of unsettling rumors about Leilon) and **Silla Scalesweep**, a young halfling musician heading to Leilon's fishery.
- **On the High Road** they broke a Talos ritual (an Anchorite, berserkers and cultists). A captured berserker raved that the storms are growing stronger along the coast, the dead are rising, **Talos is tearing "the veil" thin**, and a prophecy says **Leilon will drown beneath the coming storm**.
- **Leilon:** they arrived to a tense town meeting: strange happenings in the marshes, panicked fisherfolk, unnatural activity beyond the marsh islands, and **two missing children**. They tracked a cult wagon into the marsh and, after a hard fight against a **Cultist Elite**, cultists and **Tide Knights**, **rescued the children**. Every cultist bore a tattoo of **three parallel lightning bolts** on the forearm.
- At **The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn** and the market they heard of unnatural storms, strange lights in the marsh, disappearances and the **Thunder Cliffs**. The **town council** confirmed it all: the Thunder Cliffs once held ancient shrines to Talos, and people fear the cult has returned to rebuild them. **Grond** joined at the council meeting (session 48).
- **The midnight docks:** the party interrupted **Nixoxious**, a tiefling priestess of Talos in blue robes, and her berserkers (Daz's silence spell smothered their chanting). The ritual still summoned a **water elemental**, which Kai held off and Zeftina's magic drove back into the water.

### 6. The supply run (sessions 49–55)
- The council asked them to escort **Pinchwit Wigglehoof**, a gnome goatherder, and her giant goats to Phandalin to arrange supplies for Leilon's rebuilding. They killed a band of goat-stealing **ogres** on the way (the goats refused to budge).
- In Phandalin, Daz, Kai and Kieran were recognized as heroes. Pinchwit arranged supplies with **Elmar Barthen**; Kieran bought a **+1 rapier** at **Linene Graywind**'s Lionshield Coster, now a well-stocked armory.
- As the wagon left, the weather turned unnaturally and **Argus**, a vampire spawn serving Talos, attacked, while **Velleen**, a master thief, and her kobolds struck from the other side. Both were defeated. The kobold **Skrik** surrendered after Kieran convinced him his fey magic could read thoughts, and traveled with the party as a prisoner.
- Argus carried orders to delay the wagon until reinforcements arrived, and Velleen's map showed **someone knew exactly when the supplies would leave Phandalin.**

### 7. The lost patrol (sessions 55–60)
- Back in Leilon, the council rewarded each of them with 25 gp and **gave the party a house as a permanent base**. Zeftina set about making it a home.
- The stonemason **Breltora Red-Eye** said her friend **Vaster Jessup** (spelled "Vester" later in the recap) and his patrol were overdue in the **Mere of Dead Men**. **Sergeant Yorrum** unofficially let the party look: "Bring them home."
- In the swamp they killed three trolls, then found a **moving lizardfolk village** of about fifty under **Chief Hissain**, with eight human prisoners in cages (four from the patrol), meant as bait for the **Rot Trolls** hunting the tribe. Kieran and Zeftina, speaking Draconic, offered the party's help instead. Three Rot Trolls attacked; the party killed them while the lizardfolk guarded their families. The prisoners were freed, and Hissain declared the party welcome among the tribe. Zeftina offered to check on them from time to time.

### 8. Thalivar's Tower (sessions 60–62, ongoing)
- At the inn, Grond met **Gallio Elibro**, a ranking member of the **Order of the Many-Starred Cloak**, overseeing Neverwinter's reconstruction of an old wizard's tower (to become a military garrison). The workers had quit over strange events.
- **Thalivar**, a wizard, built the tower in Leilon about two hundred years ago to study the boundary between the mortal world and other planes, distant observation, communication and magical energy. He vanished or met with some accident; the ruin is said to be haunted.
- **Pippa** arrived in Leilon about this time, performing around town and collecting stories for a new ballad, and joined the party (session 60).
- The former workers: **Merris Thorne**, a stonemason (footsteps above, movement behind solid stone, sudden cold, tools moving); **Joren Vask**, a carpenter (a voice calling his name, a woman whispering, footsteps on the stairs; "The tower is watching us"); and **Elira Fenwick**, a salvage worker who had taken **a small metallic disc** from the tower.
- In a harbor warehouse, Elira's disc drew out an unseen **watcher**. The disc is dark metal marked like a star map, with a small black glass-like stone at its center; exposed, it chills the air and bends flame toward it. Zeftina took it. Pippa played on her flute a melody she had heard near the tower, which disturbed the entity. It said: the tower is its **window**, the disc its **key**; Thalivar opened the way and called it with the melody; the window leads to **"the places between"**; and if the disc isn't returned, **"the window will open elsewhere."** The party withdrew into daylight. Kieran brought everyone a flask of **holy water** (he lied to a priest to get it).
- Kai asked why the entity hadn't manifested in two centuries if the disc was all it needed; the party suspects another component is required.
- In the archives beneath Town Hall, **Professor Alric Vey**, a scholar who came with Gallio, found that the tower was built around a chamber called a **Point of Contact**, connected to the **Far Threshold**, for observing or communicating across a boundary. It needs **two components: one fixed in the tower, one portable.** The plans show a chamber **directly beneath the main laboratory** that current measurements say isn't there. Vey warned them not to activate anything they don't understand.
- At the tower, Gallio let them in after tense words, asking them to report anything tied to Thalivar's experiments before disturbing it. They are now exploring the ground floor. See `current-state.md`.
