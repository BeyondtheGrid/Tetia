1. Dungeon MasterAPP — 9:45 AM
Pippa’s little song echoes around the spiral stairwell. For a moment, it almost makes the tower feel less oppressive. Almost.
The Second Floor
The staircase finally reaches the next level. You emerge into a small hallway that turns sharply to the east. The passage extends roughly forty feet before opening onto a section of wooden scaffolding constructed against the exterior of the tower. Through the gaps beyond the scaffolding, you can see open sky and the surrounding landscape far below. Three closed doors line the hallway. At the very end of the passage is another room. 

Unlike the others, it has no door at all. The entrance simply stands open. Dust hangs motionless in the air. The tower is quiet again. 

The party has barely taken a few steps down the hallway when a faint sound comes from outside. Scrape. Then another. Step. The sound seems to be coming from the scaffolding. 

The groups attention immediately shifts toward the open section of the hallway. A shadow moves beyond the wooden beams. For a moment, it looks as though something is climbing along the outside of the tower. Then a figure steps into view. 

An elven woman carefully crosses one of the scaffold platforms, one hand resting against the ancient stonework for balance. She wears the vestments and equipment of a traveling cleric, though they show signs of considerable travel. A holy symbol hangs visibly at her neck. She reaches the edge of the platform and looks through the opening toward the party.

Her eyes widen. “Oh.” She glances behind herself. Then back at the adventurers. “I was beginning to wonder if I was the only person foolish enough to come inside.” She carefully steps through the opening onto the hallway. “I am Tetia Mercury.”

May I ask, in turn, whom I am addressing?"**

Tetia Mercury — posted 7 October 2026, after Kai's post (time not recorded)
The questions come quickly, with no name attached and no answer to her own. Tetia's weight settles onto her back foot, half a step nearer the sky behind her. The one who asks is a young man with dark red hair and a patch over one eye, and his ears come to a point: elven, or partly so.

_They ask as people who are searching this place, as I am. Allies, perhaps._

She answers him, each part in its order.

**"In this region, some days. As for this tower, the way I chose to enter has taken a good deal longer than I wished; I had thought to be finished and away by now. I am here independently, on the instruction of my court, and my inquiry has scarcely begun."**

Her eyes go briefly to the halfling.

**"A wizard, beyond the one who appears to stand among you? No. I have seen no one and heard no one until I found you. I believed myself alone here."** A faint crease gathers between her brows. **"Is this tower not abandoned?"**

_Perhaps what I know of this place is out of date. How much else is?_

1. Tetia MercuryAPP — 6:52 PM
At Pip's flourish and bright greeting, the crease between Tetia's brows smooths, and her weight comes forward off her back foot. Then the orc who names himself Grond looks her over, broad as a door in battered black plate, and a small, hard swallow travels down her throat. Even so she returns Pip's bow, a shade deeper than the moment asks, her armor chiming faintly. A keen eye might find no weapon anywhere on Tetia: no blade, no mace, no bow, as if she either expects no danger here or has no need of steel to meet it.

**"I am glad to meet you, Pip, since you are kind enough to offer the name; and I thank you, Grond, for yours and for so plain an answer. I am accustomed to working alone, but this tower is not deserted as I expected it to be, and I would welcome the company."**
