The battle erupts into chaos. Steel clashes against claw. Spells streak through the darkened streets. The unnatural storm churns overhead, casting the town of Phandalin beneath an ominous twilight. The party's attention is fixed on the servant of Talos and the advancing ghouls.

Then—A sharp whistle cuts through the air from the south. Not one of warning. One of command. Several small figures dart from between two buildings, moving with surprising speed. They wear mismatched scraps of leather armor and clutch an assortment of spears, slings, and rusty blades. 

Kobolds. Half a dozen or more of the reptilian creatures fan out into the street, their yellow eyes gleaming with mischief and greed as they chatter excitedly in Draconic. One points toward the supply wagon. Another toward the horses. A third grins wickedly as it readies its sling. 

Behind them strides their leader. A agile human woman dressed in dark leathers, her hood thrown back despite the rain. A pair of daggers rests comfortably in her hands, and a crooked smile spreads across her face as she surveys the battlefield. Her gaze lingers on the wagon. Then on the party. She laughs. "Well now..." She twirls one dagger between her fingers. "Looks like we picked the perfect day." 

The woman gives a casual wave toward the adventurers. "Nothing personal." She nods toward the wagon piled high with supplies. "Business is business." With another sharp whistle, she points one dagger toward the loaded wagon. The kobolds immediately spring into action, spreading through the street with practiced coordination. Some rush toward the horses. Others move to surround the wagon.

The thief herself advances confidently behind her diminutive followers, clearly intending to take advantage of the confusion created by the Talos worshippers. 

Phandalin's quiet morning has become a three-sided battle. The heroes now find themselves caught between the undead servants of Talos to the north......and an opportunisti
c band of thieves and kobold henchmen approaching from the south. The supplies bound for Leilon have suddenly become the most contested cargo in town.
