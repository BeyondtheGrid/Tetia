The first rot troll, already enraged by the party's resistance, turns its attention toward Kieran. With a sudden lunge, its jaws snap shut around him. CRUNCH! The ranger reels as the troll's teeth sink into him. Before Kieran can recover, one massive claw tears across him, raking through armor and flesh. Kieran staggers backward, but remains on his feet.

The troll follows with another swipe toward Kai. The paladin raises his guard, but the rotten claws sweep harmlessly past him. The creature snarls, frustrated that its second target has escaped its reach. 

Across the clearing, the second rot troll hauls its massive body upright. It shakes itself violently, mud and stagnant water flying from its rotting hide. Its yellow eyes settle on Grond. With a guttural roar, it charges through the muck. But the distance is too great. The troll's heavy footsteps churn the swamp as it closes the gap, yet it cannot reach the orc before the momentum of its charge carries it to a halt. 

Then comes another sound from deeper within the Mere. A roar. The party turns just in time to see a third rot troll emerge from the reeds. The creature lumbers toward the clearing, its grotesque body covered in decaying flesh and dripping slime. It has joined the battle. Fortunately, it is still too far away to strike.

Hissain's warriors tighten their defensive formation as the new threat appears. The chief raises his staff. "Hold!" The lizardfolk obey. They remain focused on protecting their people while the three monstrous trolls close in on the adventurers.

The odds have just become considerably worse. But the tribe is still counting on the heroes.

Hissain watches the last of the Rot Trolls for a moment before turning his attention back to Kieran. “For a time,” he replies in Draconic. “The Rot Trolls have hunted us for many seasons. These three were not the only ones that stalk the Mere.” He glances toward the sprawling marsh surrounding the village. “But three fewer trolls hunt my people now. That is more safety than we had this morning.”

Hissain then looks toward his tribe, where the lizardfolk are helping one another and tending to those injured in the attack. “We will move before the scent of their blood draws others. We are travelers. We know how to disappear into the Mere.” A faint, toothy smile crosses his face. “And should another troll find us... perhaps it will think twice after hearing what happened here.” 

He looks back to Kieran. “You have our thanks, hunter. Your people are welcome beside ours, should you ever have need of us.”
