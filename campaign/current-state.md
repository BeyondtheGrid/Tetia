## The scene right now
- **The second floor.** The spiral stair comes up into a small hallway that turns sharply east and runs about forty feet to a section of wooden scaffolding built against the outside of the tower; open sky and the land far below show through the gaps. **Three closed doors** line the hallway. At the far end is **another room with no door at all**, its entrance standing open. Dust hangs motionless in the air. The tower is quiet.
- **Tetia's entrance (written by the GM):** the party had taken a few steps down the hallway when they heard a scrape and a step on the scaffolding and saw a shadow that looked like something climbing the outside of the tower. An elven woman crossed a scaffold platform with one hand on the stonework: the vestments and equipment of a traveling cleric, showing considerable travel, a holy symbol at her neck. Her eyes widened. "Oh." She glanced behind her, then back. "I was beginning to wonder if I was the only person foolish enough to come inside." She stepped through the opening into the hallway. "I am Tetia Mercury."
- **Zeftina** had readied a spell at the sound of steps: a blazing clock hand of fire in her grasp, held back as the elf approached. "What are you doing in this tower?"
- **Tetia's reply (her first post):** she stopped one step inside the hallway, her shield lifting a few inches before she made herself lower it, the color draining from her face, her gaze on the flame. She said that she had not expected to meet anyone, least of all anyone who would greet her with fire; that she is a researcher at the court of Sarifal, in the Moonshae Isles, sent to observe the storms upon this coast; that she is told voices are heard in this tower with no one there to speak them, which can be the mark of a place where two worlds lie close together, her field of study. She asked whom she is addressing.
- **Waiting on:** the party's and the GM's replies. As far as the record shows, Zeftina's spell is still held, and nobody has given Tetia a name.
- **Positions** (worked out from the GM's description): Tetia is at the east end of the hallway, one step inside the opening from the scaffolding. The party is near the stair at the other end, a few steps in.

## The tower so far
- **Outside:** a ruin on a crag; the top floor soot-blackened and cracked open like an egg; a fissure down one side behind new scaffolding; abandoned timbers, stone, ropes and tools; no guards, no workers. Two ruined arches lead in, and stairs lead up into the entry hall.
- **Entry hall:** fallen masonry, puddles of muddy rainwater, starlings in the eaves, dim gray light. A banquet table holds **eight soldiers' packs**, set down neatly as if their owners meant to return. Most bear a small **blue-and-silver shield** on the flap, the mark of the soldiers assigned to the reconstruction detail. Several have been rummaged through, but nothing was stolen.
  - Grond opened one: **three healing potions, five healer's kits and 123 gold**, carefully gathered.
  - No names; a few scratched initials and faded symbols.
  - Pip noticed one pack with a **clean line through its dust**, as if something recently brushed it without disturbing it.
  - None of the people the party questioned ever mentioned the packs.
  - **The party left everything where it was** for now (Kieran and Zeftina refused to take any; Grond: "We'll grab them on the way out").
- **East hallway, first door on the right:** a servant's chamber (bed collapsed under rubble), and through it the kitchen, buried in masonry dust. The rooms feel **interrupted**, as though whoever lived and worked here simply stopped one day and never came back. The second doorway off the east hallway hasn't been described.
- **North door: Gallio's study.** Kieran checked for traps before Grond opened it.
  - A surprisingly intact room: tall shelves of musty scrolls, journals and old volumes, many brought down from the tower's **library on the second floor**. **Gallio was inside**, at a large desk, deciphering Thalivar's coded notes.
  - **What Pip read over his shoulder:** the notes mix arcane symbols, diagrams and cipher, and look less like spell formulae than **schematics**. Gallio has drawn, several times, the same circular symbol that is carved into the masonry beneath the Town Hall archives (and resembles the pattern on the disc). Beside one he has deciphered **"POINT OF CONTACT"**. Underlined several times: **"The boundary is not a wall. It is a threshold."**
  - Gallio turned guarded and asked why everyone who investigates the tower finds pieces of the same puzzle while nobody knows the whole picture. Pip pointedly asked why he would withhold information from the people helping him.
  - **The party is suspicious of him.** He was outside when they went in, and then he was in a room inside. Zeftina whispered it; Kieran asked, "Gallio, how long have you been at this table?" with his hand drifting toward his rapier; Kai noted Gallio seems comfortable operating from inside the tower; Pip: one Gallio outside and one inside, or one Gallio who can teleport? **His answer isn't in the record** (see the gap above).

## What the party knows
**Tetia knows none of this** until someone tells her in play.
- **Thalivar** built the tower about two hundred years ago to study the boundary between worlds, distant observation and communication, then vanished or met with an accident.
- **The disc:** palm-sized, dark tarnished metal with a strange silvery surface beneath, perfectly smooth edges; fine markings like a star map around a shallow depression; at its center a small, completely black glass-like stone in which something seems to move, like a reflection of something impossibly distant. Exposed, it chills the air and draws flame toward it. Elira found it **"on the floor upstairs"** in the tower. **Zeftina carries it** in her pack, wrapped. They have not shown it to anyone.
- **The watcher**, met in Elira's warehouse:
  - It appeared as a tall, thin humanoid shape, but wrong: its silhouette bends where no body should, and its hand has too many fingers. For a moment the party saw behind it **a vast darkness filled with stars**.
  - It speaks in a woman's voice: "I am the one who watched. The one who listened." / "I was the watcher." "The tower was my window. The disc was my key." "Thalivar opened the way." "The song was how he called me." "Return it to the tower, or the window will open elsewhere."
  - **Pip's melody** (heard near the tower one night) disturbed it. It begged her to stop and vanished.
  - When the disc pulsed, **thunder rolled across a clear morning sky**.
  - Elira said the voice was **not the one she heard in the tower**.
- **Professor Vey** (in the archives beneath Town Hall), researching for the Order of the Many-Starred Cloak:
  - The tower was built around a **Point of Contact** linked to the **Far Threshold**, a place Thalivar believed "could be observed but not safely reached." Thalivar called it "the space between stars," "the place beyond the veil," "the distant observatory."
  - It needs **two components**, one fixed in the tower and one portable that establishes or maintains the connection. The plans show a chamber directly beneath the main laboratory that current measurements say isn't there.
  - If it was built only to communicate, restoring it might let something on the other side talk to us. If it allows passage, the problem is much larger.
  - An exposed recess in the archive wall bears a carved circular symbol like the disc's pattern.
  - Vey hasn't entered the tower. He asked them to come to him before activating anything, and "if you find something you don't understand... don't touch it." His eyes lingered on Zeftina's pack.
- **Each party member carries a flask of holy water** (Kieran bought them from the team fund).
- **Kai's question:** if the disc alone were enough, why hasn't the watcher come through in two centuries? The party suspects another piece is missing. Kieran and Kai want the disc kept away from their house and the town.
