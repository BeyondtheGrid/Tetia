## What the party knows about the tower
- Thalivar, a wizard, built it about two hundred years ago to study the boundary between the mortal world and other planes, distant observation and communication. He vanished, or met with an accident.
- It was built around a chamber called a **Point of Contact**, connected to the **Far Threshold**. It needs **two components: one fixed in the tower, one portable.**
- The plans show **a chamber directly beneath the main laboratory** that current measurements say doesn't exist.
- **The disc** (the portable component, it seems): dark metal marked like a star map, with a small black glass-like stone at its center. Exposed, it drops the temperature and bends flame toward it. **The party has it**; Zeftina took it from Elira.
- **The watcher:** an unseen entity that speaks. It calls the tower its window and the disc its key, says Thalivar opened the way and called it with a melody, says the window leads to "the places between," and warns that if the disc isn't returned, "the window will open elsewhere."
- **The melody:** Pippa heard it near the tower and played it; it disturbed the entity.
- Workers reported footsteps, movement and crying behind solid stone, sudden cold, tools moving, and a voice calling names. Nothing has attacked anyone.
- Each party member carries a flask of holy water.
