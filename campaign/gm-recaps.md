Session One - July 27 – August 3
The journey to Phandalin took a hard turn not long ago.

Ambushed on the trail by a band of goblins, you fought for your lives—steel against rusted scimitars, arrows cutting through tall grass. You stood your ground. When the dust settled, the goblins lay dead around you… and one new figure stood nearby:

Clover, a lone traveler who had witnessed the battle and rushed in to help. Too late for the fight, but not too late to become an ally.

Together, you continued down the road.

It was just before reaching Phandalin—on a high, grassy ridge—that you saw it:  A white dragon, distant but unmistakable, gliding across the sky like a storm given wings. Silent. Cold. Indifferent.

The moment passed. The dragon vanished behind the clouds. You descended into the valley and entered the town.

After a brief stop at the Stonehill Inn—a warm fire, a drink, and a few wary glances from locals—you turned back. The memory of the ambush fresh in your minds. Goblins don’t strike travelers that boldly unless they’re working from nearby. A cave. A camp. A nest.

And so, now, you return to the scene of the ambush, eyes sharp, weapons ready—not for battle this time, but for signs. Tracks. Trails. Clues.

The bodies are still where you left them, crows already circling overhead. The wind stirs the tall grass. Somewhere out there, the rest of the goblins are waiting.

Time to find them.
