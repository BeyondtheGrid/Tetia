# Table Rules and Post Format

**Source:** the GM's rules, supplied by the director on 5 October 2026. Part 1 is the GM's text, lightly tidied. Part 2 is how those rules apply to Tetia's posts, and **it is the authority for post formatting.** Where the GM later clarifies or changes something, update both parts and note it in the changelog.

---

## Part 1: The GM's rules

### General
- **Be respectful.** This is a game and everyone wants to have fun. Harassing, disrespecting or being intentionally mean to a player or DM is grounds for removal. This applies to out-of-character talk; in-character conflict is fine as long as it isn't a veiled attack on a player.
- **Time zone:** the game runs on Central Standard Time (CST, Chicago). All times the DM mentions are CST.
- **Start Playing:** everyone must be signed up for this game on Start Playing. Skipping a week there moves you off the active list: you keep the general channel but lose the game channels.
- **Fees:** collected automatically every Sunday at 12:30 PM CST through Start Playing.
- **Rulings:** the DM has final say. Rules can be sent to the DM for review.
- **DM posts:** about 3 times a day.
- **Minimum posts:** at least once a week, or risk removal. Once a day is strongly preferred. Tell the DM if you'll be unavailable.
- **Weekly check-in:** at the start of each new session the DM checks how the game is going and whether anything needs adjusting.
- **No editing story (in-character) posts without the DM's permission.**
- **Pace of game:** to be decided.

### House rules
1. No PvP. The party works as a group.
2. **Luck rolls:** a straight d20 whose result can shape events (for example, whether a guard is there when you sneak somewhere).
3. Healing potions are a bonus action and heal for full value.
4. Material components are ignored, except for special spells such as *Wish* and spells that bring characters back to life.
5. Players track their own inventory, on trust.
6. Flanking gives advantage (the 2014 rule).
7. Critical hits: maximum damage plus a roll. The DM adjusts Avrae's hit points to match.
8. Inspiration is awarded for good roleplay or clever thinking.
9. Anything can be a mimic.

### Posting guidelines
- **Narration and action:** plain text.
- **Speech:** in quotation marks, preferably bold.
- **Thoughts:** underscores, for internal thoughts.
- **Dice:** use `!roll` with Avrae, or post your D&D Beyond roll results.

### Combat posts
- Include your action, movement, bonus action, reactions and so on.
- A short roleplay blurb plus the command. The GM's example: *Gorrim growls and swings his axe* `!attack greataxe`
- If rolling manually, roll attack and damage together to speed things up, for example:
  - Longbow attack: `!roll 1d20+5`
  - Damage: `!roll 1d8+3`
- **Turn timer:** post your turn within 24 hours of being tagged. Otherwise the DM skips you and moves on; you can take the missed turn when you return. If it still isn't taken by your next turn, the DM may move your character with a basic action or have them Dodge.
- **Maps:** shared as images or through online tools as needed.

### Out of character
- Table talk and clarifications go in the **#ooc** channel.

### Final notes
- Questions are welcome anytime.
- Play-by-post takes patience and commitment, but it can be a deeply rewarding, story-rich experience.

## Part 2: What this means for Tetia's posts

### The format
| Element | Discord markup | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Narration and action | Plain text. **No italics.** | She lowers the book and looks toward the door. |
| Speech | Bold, inside quotation marks | `**"Forgive me. I did not hear you come in."**` |
| Thoughts | Single underscores (Discord italics) | `_So this is a tavern._` |
| Telepathy | Bold, inside angle brackets, so it reads as speech but not spoken aloud *(Claude's default; the GM's guide doesn't cover it)* | `**‹Can you hear me? Do not turn around.›**` |
| Sign language | Formatted as speech; the narration says she signs it | She signs, small and quick: `**"Two guards. Left side."**` |
| Dice and commands | Never in the story post. The director rolls in a separate channel | |

- **Thoughts:** use a single underscore on each side. Double underscores (`__like this__`) underline in Discord instead. Check this against how the table posts in the Discord log, and switch if the table does otherwise.
- **Telepathy:** never use underscores or italics for it, or it will read as a private thought. The narration should make clear the words arrive in the listener's mind.
- **Person and tense:** third person, present tense, matching the GM's example ("Gorrim growls and swings his axe") and the table's practice.
- **No out-of-character notes in story posts.** Table talk goes in #ooc. If the director wants an OOC note, draft it separately and label it for #ooc.
- **Length limit:** a Discord message holds 2,000 characters (more with Nitro). If a post runs longer, split it at a paragraph break and say where the split goes.
- **Handover:** give the director the post inside a code block, so the bold and underscores survive copying into Discord.

### What the table actually does
Observed in the August 2026 chat (`campaign/chat/2026-08-wagon-battle.md`). **Where this differs from the GM's written guide, follow the table's practice**, and the director can confirm.
- **Posts go out under the character's name** with Discord's APP tag, which means a bot or webhook posts them: a character-proxy bot such as Tupperbox. The GM posts as "Dungeon Master" and voices NPCs under their own names (for example, "Kobold").
- **Story posts are narrative only.** No dice commands, no movement/action/bonus action lines, no out-of-character notes. Rolls are made in a separate channel; the story post is the narration of the turn.
- **Third person, present tense:** confirmed.
- **Speech:** in quotation marks, often on a line of its own. Whether the table bolds it can't be seen, because copying from Discord strips formatting; keep bolding it, as the GM's guide asks.
- **Players narrate their own results** (hits, misses, kills), since the rolls are already made; the GM narrates the enemies and the scene. For Tetia, results come from the director: a kill only when he says the creature dies.
- **Some players name their abilities** ("the Crimson Rite," "a fireball"). Tetia's posts still don't (the director's rule).
- **Calling out to allies is normal** ("On your six, Tate!"). Speaking to another PC is fine; writing their reply or reaction is not.
- **Length:** player combat posts run about 30 to 150 words, most of them 50 to 100. GM posts run 150 to 400.

### Combat posts
- **Narrative only by default:** what she does, how it looks and feels, anything she says. Show the act in the fiction; never name the mechanic.
- **Outcomes:** narrate hits, misses and effects only as the director reports them from the rolls. If he hasn't said, leave the result open.
- **Short:** aim for the table's usual 50 to 100 words unless the director asks otherwise.
- **The director rolls in a separate channel** and tells Claude the results (hit or miss, saves made or failed). Claude never writes commands or mechanical summaries.

### Rules that shape the writing
- **No edits after posting** without the DM's permission, so the draft must be right before the director posts it. Check continuity and formatting before handing it over.
- **Material components are ignored.** Never show her fumbling for components. This already fits her: her magic flows through her body and her amulet.
- **Healing potions are a bonus action.** If she drinks one in combat, a quick swallow, not a scene.
- **No PvP.** She never acts against another player character.
- **Luck rolls and all other outcomes belong to the GM and the dice.** Write what she attempts; describe a result only when the director reports it.
- **Inspiration rewards good roleplay and clever thinking.** Posts should give her real character moments, within the length the director asks for.
- **Pacing:** the GM posts about 3 times a day; players must post at least weekly and ideally daily; in combat, within 24 hours of being tagged. All GM times are CST.
- "Anything can be a mimic" is table humor. Tetia knows of mimics only as far as her books would.
