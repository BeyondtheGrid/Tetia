# The Party

**What this is:** the other player characters, as their players have presented them. Art is in `campaign/art/`.

**Rules for using this file**
- **Never control another PC.** Don't write their actions, words, thoughts or reactions in Tetia's posts. Only what they have already posted can be referred to, and only as it happened.
- **Character intros are table knowledge, not Tetia's knowledge.** She knows only what she has seen, heard or been told in play. On first meeting she knows only what she can see.
- Players' own descriptions are quoted exactly. Appearance notes from art are tagged *(art)*; anything worked out from those is tagged *(derived)*.
- Update each entry as the log reveals more: what Tetia has learned about them, and how their relationship with her develops.
- **These five are the whole party** besides Tetia (confirmed by the director, 5 October 2026). None of them were part of the original caravan party; each joined later (session numbers below). Earlier companions, including Tate and Daz, are listed in `npcs.md`.

### The player's description
> Pippa is a cheerful, charismatic gnome who loves music, good stories, dramatic entrances, and an audience. She is genuinely kind-hearted, fiercely loyal, and has a particular soft spot for anyone being overlooked, mistreated, or underestimated. She has a knack for making people smile, even when they don't particularly want to. She believes everyone has a story worth hearing, especially the people who might otherwise be forgotten. Pip is forgiving and believes most people deserve a second chance. But beneath her sunny personality is a sharper side that comes out when something she cares deeply about is threatened or harmed. She isn't cruel or eager for revenge, but she has a remarkable talent for figuring out exactly what would make someone regret crossing her.
>
> She'll happily be the life of the party, but she's also the little gnome you want standing beside you when things go sideways.
>
> Pippa believes "Most deserve a second chance. Some shouldn't get a third."

### Appearance
- **Build:** small, as gnomes are; Tetia, at 5'10", stands roughly twice her height *(derived)*.
- **Hair:** a mass of curly copper-red hair, partly pulled back with small braids at the crown, and two long braids falling over her shoulders *(art)*.
- **Face:** large bright green eyes, freckles across the nose and cheeks, rosy cheeks, a warm, mischievous smile; pointed ears *(art)*.
- **Jewelry:** teal drop earrings and a teal pendant at her throat *(art)*.
- **Clothes:** a cream peasant blouse with full, gathered sleeves; layered skirts in teal, green and cream with gold embroidery and ragged hems; a flowing teal cloak; leather belts hung with pouches and small charms, including crescent moons; worn brown buckled boots *(art)*.
- **Instrument:** a wooden lute with a carved floral design, feathers and beads hanging from the pegbox *(art)*.
- **Gear:** a leather pack with books and a bedroll *(art)*.
- **Magic:** in the art, golden musical notes swirl around her as she plays *(art)*.

### In play so far (from the GM's recaps)
- **Joined:** session 60. She had recently arrived in Leilon, performing around town and collecting stories for a new ballad.
- Heard a strange melody near Thalivar's Tower several nights before; in the warehouse she played it **on her flute**, and it disturbed the watcher.

### The player's description
> Grond was born among the Oakfell Clan, a nomadic tribe of orcs devoted to Luthic. Years ago, raiders attacked his people, leaving many dead and scattering the survivors across the frontier.
>
> Badly wounded and left for dead, Grond survived through grit, determination, and what he believes was a sign from Luthic herself: a single command.
>
> "Live."
>
> Since then, he has wandered as a hunter, laborer, and sellsword, helping where he can and searching for purpose beyond survival. His travels have now brought him to Phandalin, where rumors of monsters and a white dragon threaten the region.
>
> Grond seeks neither glory nor riches. He simply believes that when danger comes for good people, someone must stand in its way.

### Appearance
- **Build:** big, broad and heavily muscled; orcs commonly stand 6 to 7 feet *(lore)*, so he is likely taller than Tetia and far heavier *(derived)*.
- **Face:** green, weathered skin, lined and pitted; a heavy brow; dark, steady eyes; two lower tusks jutting up past his lip; pointed ears; a full black beard *(art)*.
- **Hair:** long and black, swept back from his face and falling past his shoulders *(art)*.
- **Armor:** heavy, blackened plate, battered and pitted from use, with large layered pauldrons, fur trim at the collar, shoulders and waist, broad leather belts with a big square buckle, and a dark red cloth hanging below the waist *(art)*.
- **Weapon:** a single-bladed axe with a long, cord-wrapped haft *(art)*.
- **Shield:** a large round shield of dark metal with a riveted rim, painted with a pale, branching sigil. The same sigil appears faintly on his breastplate and on a banner behind him in the art *(art)*. What it means is open.

### Faith: Luthic *(lore)*
- Luthic is the orc goddess of fertility, healing, the home and caves, called the Cave Mother; she is the consort of Gruumsh.
- A cleric with Tetia's religious learning could well recognize the name. What she actually knows or thinks of Luthic is open until it comes up in play.

### In play so far (from the GM's recaps)
- **Joined:** session 48, pledging his strength at Leilon's council meeting against the cultists.
- Fought the Talos berserkers at the docks, the ogres and the Rot Trolls.
- At the inn, overheard Gallio Elibro and brought the tower job to the party (he called the Order "the Order of the Starry Wishes").
- In the warehouse, ordered everyone toward the exit and stood between the party and the darkness, axe ready and shield raised.

### In the chat (August 2026)
- **Fights with:** an enchanted battleaxe and his shield, which he uses to bash, knock down and shove foes. Charges in bursts of speed; shakes off his wounds mid-fight.
- **Speech:** terse and gruff. "Get away from them!" "Your turn!" "Stay down." "Where do you think you're going?" To the captured kobold: "No. Just your life. So... start talking."

### Open

### The player's description
> Shortened Backstory:
> Blessed at birth by an archfey, thanks to his parents' protection of the forest and its creatures, Kieran grew up in the forest in Eberron. He and his two closest friends played all the time in the woods before growing older and wanting adventure. They explored much of Eberron before being sent for more adventure through a planar portal to Faerun.
>
> His friends left to live in the High Forest and he chose to explore museums and ancient runes to find lost artifacts and the secrets of this land. He eventually fell in love with the beauty of the sword coast and its people, and, after learning of the Harpers, wants more than anything to become one.
>
> Most recently, he found himself in Phandalin, where he took a contract to find some missing loggers before running into some trouble.

### Appearance
- **Overall:** a lean man who looks human *(art)*.
- **Face:** warm brown skin; dark, intense eyes under heavy dark brows; a straight nose; a thin mustache and short chin beard over light stubble; a steady, guarded expression *(art)*.
- **Hair:** dark, mostly hidden under his hood *(art)*.
- **Clothes:** a deep green hooded cloak of rough, heavy cloth with frayed edges, wrapped high around his shoulders and worn with the hood up; an olive-green tunic beneath; dark leather gloves or bracers *(art)*.
- **Gear:** a quiver of dark-fletched arrows on his back; a worn brown leather strap across his chest with metal fittings; leather belts with buckles and pouches *(art)*.

### Kalashtar *(lore)*
- A people from the world of Eberron: humans each bound to a spirit from the plane of dreams. They look human, though often with an otherworldly composure, and their eyes may glow when they use their psionic power.
- They have a limited power of telepathy.
- Kalashtar are essentially unknown in Faerûn. To Tetia he would look like a human man until she learns otherwise *(derived)*.

### Fey Wanderer *(lore)*
- A ranger touched by the Feywild, often carrying some visible or felt trace of it. Any trace Kieran carries is the player's to describe.

### Common ground with Tetia
Facts only, for the writer's awareness; what comes of them is for play to decide.
- Both can speak mind to mind.
- He was blessed by an archfey and carries the Feywild's touch; she is a scholar of the Feywild from a fey court.
- He hopes to join the Harpers.

### In play so far (from the GM's recaps)
- **Joined:** session 31, at the Logger's Camp, having tracked the burrowing creatures.
- Shot the geomancer with a longbow; arrows throughout the party's battles. Bought a +1 rapier in Phandalin.
- Convinced the kobold Skrik that his fey magic could read thoughts.
- Speaks Draconic; led the peaceful approach to the lizardfolk.
- Lied to a priest to get holy water for everyone, then explained that drinking it does nothing unless you're undead.
- Suggested keeping the disc away from the party's house and from Leilon.

### In the chat (August 2026)
- **Fights with:** a longbow, two arrows a turn. He marks a target first; tiny illusions of **flowers and fairies** dance through his arrows when he infuses them with fey magic.
- **Moves by vanishing into mist** and reappearing elsewhere. Carries rope.
- **Speech:** quick and practical. Calls out to allies ("On your six, Tate!") and offers enemies a way out ("It's not too late to run and live!"). Threatened the kobold in Draconic.

### Open

- What Tetia knows of Eberron, if anything, and of the Harpers.
