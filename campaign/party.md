# The Party

**What this is:** the index to the other player characters, as their players have presented them. Each has a file of their own in `campaign/party/`, and art in `campaign/art/`.

**How it loads:** the **party roster** in `campaign/current-state.md` is loaded in every session. It says who each of them is at a glance, that they are allies, and what Tetia herself knows of each. Read a character's full file only when that character matters to the post: the director mentions a detail about them, or Tetia addresses, examines or reacts to them in particular.

**Rules for using these files**
- **Never control another PC.** Don't write their actions, words, thoughts or reactions in Tetia's posts. Only what they have already posted can be referred to, and only as it happened.
- **Character intros are table knowledge, not Tetia's knowledge.** She knows only what she has seen, heard or been told in play. On first meeting she knows only what she can see.
- Players' own descriptions are quoted exactly. Appearance notes from art are tagged *(art)*; anything worked out from those is tagged *(derived)*.
- **One home per fact.** What Tetia knows of each of them *right now* lives in the roster. Lasting moments between them go under "With Tetia (milestones)" in their file, added at milestones. New details a player reveals (appearance, abilities, history) go in that character's file.
- **These five are the whole party** besides Tetia (confirmed by the director, 5 October 2026). None of them were part of the original caravan party; each joined later. Earlier companions, including Tate and Daz, are listed in `npcs.md`.

## Tetia joining the party: her starting stance
How Tetia is likely to carry herself with these five at first, worked out from her canon (`character/bible.md`, especially sections 3, 4, 5 and 14). **A starting point, not a script:** what actually happens in play overrides it, and none of it decides how the others feel about her.
- **A newcomer to a bonded group.** She knows none of their names, history or deeds. She observes first, asks many questions, answers few about home, and records each of them in her journal.
- **Formal from the start:** full names and courtesy titles, and she asks how each would like to be addressed. Moving to a first name is a step she asks permission for.
- **Arm's length.** She keeps a courteous distance from all of them until trust is earned. Who she lets stand close, and when, is how her trust shows.
- **Size and strength put her on guard** until she knows the person; it is never contempt. Small folk don't trigger it, and she takes care not to loom over them herself, knowing how it feels.
- **Healer's eye:** she notices wounds, fatigue and old injuries at once. Courtesy keeps her from asking about them uninvited, but she will offer care.
- **Mortals are new to her.** She has rarely met humans, and she judges people by conduct, not by kind or look.
