### The player's description
> Pippa is a cheerful, charismatic gnome who loves music, good stories, dramatic entrances, and an audience. She is genuinely kind-hearted, fiercely loyal, and has a particular soft spot for anyone being overlooked, mistreated, or underestimated. She has a knack for making people smile, even when they don't particularly want to. She believes everyone has a story worth hearing, especially the people who might otherwise be forgotten. Pip is forgiving and believes most people deserve a second chance. But beneath her sunny personality is a sharper side that comes out when something she cares deeply about is threatened or harmed. She isn't cruel or eager for revenge, but she has a remarkable talent for figuring out exactly what would make someone regret crossing her.
>
> She'll happily be the life of the party, but she's also the little gnome you want standing beside you when things go sideways.
>
> Pippa believes "Most deserve a second chance. Some shouldn't get a third."

- **Clothes:** a cream peasant blouse with full, gathered sleeves; layered skirts in teal, green and cream with gold embroidery and ragged hems; a flowing teal cloak; leather belts hung with pouches and small charms, including crescent moons; worn brown buckled boots *(art)*.

### In the chat (September–October 2026)
- **Joined** when the party met her in Leilon; she had heard the strange melody passing the tower late one night. Tate and Zeftina were both a little wary of her timing.
- **Carries both a flute and a lute.** She plays the flute for tunes and inspiration (a cheerful welcome tune, a suspenseful little tune while Kieran checked for traps, a bold fanfare before the tower) and plucks the lute to steady an ally.
- **Sharp eye:** spotted the carved circular symbol in the archive wall, the clean line in the dust on one of the soldiers' packs, and the repeated symbol and "POINT OF CONTACT" in Gallio's notes.
- **Speech:** bright, playful and theatrical ("rumors with receipts"; "my phantom orchestra"), with an edge when she suspects someone is hiding something: she asked Gallio pointedly why he would withhold information from the people helping him.

### Tetia's first impressions (starting point until play decides)
- **Familiar ground:** gnomes live in Karador among the fey, so a forest gnome is one of the most familiar kinds of people Tetia could meet on the Sword Coast. Pip may put her at ease sooner than anyone else.
- **What catches her eye:** the music. A performer's warmth and drama are foreign to Llewyrr reserve, and Tetia will watch it with open fascination.
- **Closeness:** small and plainly friendly, Pip doesn't trigger her wariness of size. Tetia is careful not to loom; she may kneel to speak with her.
- **Likely friction, gentle:** Pip collects stories, and Tetia asks many questions but answers few about home. Pip may also be the one to tease out her rare, covered laugh, and to fluster her doing it.
- **Curiosity:** if she learns of the melody that disturbed the watcher, she will want to hear it and understand it.
