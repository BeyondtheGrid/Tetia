## Places

### Leilon
- **The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn:** warm food, strong drink, curious locals and an enormous stuffed umber hulk head above the bar. Professor Vey has been staying here.
- **Town Hall:** where the council meets; the records office and the old archives lie beneath it.
- **The party's house:** given by the council as a permanent base.
- **The temple of Lathander:** the lone surviving stone temple.
- **The town's reconstruction:** an unfinished palisade; the front gate where the watch drills; the docks leading into the marsh; the old warehouse district near the harbor; a market.
- **Thalivar's Tower:** on the summit of a crag near the center of town. Its upper floor is soot-blackened and cracked open, a massive fissure runs down one side, and new scaffolding stands where restoration began.

### Around Leilon
- **The Mere of Dead Men:** the swamp south along the High Road, home to lizardfolk and the Rot Trolls. The patrol route ran past an abandoned watchtower and along raised boardwalks.
- **The marsh and marsh islands:** strange lights and unnatural activity in the waters.
- **The Thunder Cliffs:** once home to ancient shrines to Talos.
- **The Wayside Inn:** on the High Road north of Leilon.

### Around Phandalin
Stonehill Inn, Barthen's Provisions, the Lionshield Coster, Umbrage Hill, the Dwarven Excavation, Butterskull Ranch, Conyberry and the Shrine of Savras, the Woodland Manse and the Circle of Thunder in Neverwinter Wood, Mountain's Toe Mine, Axeholm (now Phandalin's refuge), Tibor Wester's Logger's Camp, Dragon Barrow, and Icespire Hold.
