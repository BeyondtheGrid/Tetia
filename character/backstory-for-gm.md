# Backstory for the GM

Ronnie asked each player for a roughly one-page backstory covering the character's motivation for adventuring and what they hope to accomplish, sent as a message or in their private channel. This is what was drafted for Tetia, split into two Discord messages to stay under the 2,000-character limit.

- **Status:** drafted 5 October 2026; not yet confirmed as sent. Update this line when the director sends it.
- The hook options are suggestions to the GM, not canon. Whatever the GM chooses becomes canon once it happens in play.
- Everything here matches `bible.md`; the hook uses only what the players know from the recaps.

## Message 1: Backstory

**Tetia Mercury: Backstory**

Tetia (TEH-tee-uh) is a young Llewyrr elf, one of the reclusive elves of the Moonshae Isles, born in Synnoria, the hidden mountain kingdom on Gwynneth. At about 110 she is barely an adult by elven reckoning: a century of learning, and almost no life of her own.

When she was a child, her parents, Suletta and Brandoriel, died fighting the Unseelie fey in the service of Sarifal, the fey realm that now rules Gwynneth. Owing them that debt, Sarifal's court at Karador took her in as its ward. Her schooling raised her more than any person did, and when it ended, books filled the space. The court made her a researcher of the Feywild: fey courts, planar crossings, the places where worlds touch. She has walked in the Feywild itself, yet she has rarely met a human, never handled her own money, and never had to make her own way.

She is a cleric of the Earthmother, the Moonshaes' spirit of nature and the Balance, and her gifts run to knowledge, insight and healing rather than war. She carries no weapon.

**Why she adventures**
Prince Araithe has begun opening Sarifal to the outside world, and his niece, Lady Siofra, oversees the Sarifal Faithful, whose Llewyrr emissaries now venture into mortal lands. Lately, violent storms along the Sword Coast have been disrupting Moonshae sea trade. Tetia was sent to observe what is happening near Leilon and report back. She knows nothing of what is causing them.

**What she hopes for**
- To learn what lies behind the storms and report faithfully.
- To be worthy of her parents' sacrifice and the court's trust.
- Privately, to see mortal life up close, though she would never let that outrank her duty.
- Unspoken, even to herself: to discover what she wants, not only what she owes.

## Message 2: Suggested hook

**Suggested hook** (built only from what we players know; entirely your call)
Her first sea voyage landed her wherever the storms allowed (Waterdeep or Neverwinter, your pick), and she came up the High Road to Leilon, her first taste of a mortal town, gathering whatever talk she can about the storms.

A few ways she could meet the party:
1. **At Thalivar's Tower.** A haunted tower whose builder studied the boundary between worlds is exactly her field. She comes to see it for herself, and Gallio lets her follow the adventurers already inside.
2. **Through Professor Vey.** A visiting scholar digging through old records beneath Town Hall is the first person she would seek out. He could send her after the party.
3. **Later, in town.** If you'd rather not interrupt the tower, she could find the party afterward, at the Umber Hulk's Shell or through the council, who might point a foreign emissary toward the people already dealing with the storms.

**Good to know**
- She doesn't know the party, their history, or the Cult of Talos. Happy for her to learn it all in play.
- Speaks Common, Elvish, Sylvan and Common Sign Language, and can speak mind to mind.
- Formal, gentle, very sheltered and endlessly curious. Built for utility and healing, not fighting.
