# Homeland Reference — Synnoria, Sarifal and the Moonshae Isles

**For:** Tetia Mercury's origin. Lore reference only; what Tetia herself believes or remembers goes in `bible.md`.
**Compiled:** 2026-10-05
**Caution:** Official sources say little about Synnoria after the 1370s DR. Anything not covered below is open for the director and GM to decide; don't present invented details as established Realms lore.

## Primary source: *Moonshae Isles Regional Guide* (2018)

Adventurers League regional guide for the Season of the Rising Shadows (Baldman Games, DMs Guild), led by Shawn Merwin, who also co-wrote *Storm Lord's Wrath*. It is the most recent and detailed Moonshae source, set in the same era. **Where it conflicts with the older wiki-based notes further down, this section wins** unless the director or GM rules otherwise.

### The Llewyrr (pronounced HLEH-weer)
- Originally moon elves of what is now Neverwinter Wood. They fled the Crown Wars by sea toward Evermeet; a storm drove them onto the Moonshaes nearly 10,000 years ago, and the leShay let them settle in the mountains of Gwynneth (Synnoria).
- **Appearance:** moon-elf build, but traditionally **snow-white skin and golden hair.**
- Traditionally vegetarian; no meat at all.
- The gender imbalance that once troubled them has nearly vanished since the fey returned, and **their numbers have risen sharply in recent years.**
- **Faith: the Llewyrr do not worship Corellon or the Seldarine.** They venerate **the Earthmother, her children, and the archfey who watch over the islands.**
- They have sworn fealty to High Lady Ordalf of Sarifal.
- They are isolationists: Chrysalis lies hidden under illusions. **Very recently, some Llewyrr have begun appearing in Moonshae towns. They talk only of culture, ask many questions, answer none, and share nothing about Chrysalis's politics or economy.**
- They are known for crystal artwork; a Llewyrr crystal prism is a typical keepsake.
- From the Llewyrr Wanderer background: Llewyrr travelers are curious about the world or "sent to discover the state of the Moonshae Isles." People stare; the powerful want to meet them, hoping the Llewyrr are finally coming out of seclusion. Suggested traits include being more reserved in showing emotion than others, bathing daily in natural water, and being comfortable close to people but disliking being touched.

### Sarifal now
- Sarifal returned to Gwynneth about a century ago. High Lady Ordalf did it because of a prophecy: she is fated to die, her death must happen in the mortal world, and it will end a threat to both her people and mortals.
- **Prince Araithe** leads Sarifal's forces in Winterglen against the Unseelie of **Citadel Umbra** (ruled by the mist hag Urphania). He doesn't know why his mother brought Sarifal back.
- **Relations with mortals are warming.** House Kendrick seeks an alliance with Sarifal. **Tarilyn Kendrick, High King Derid's eldest daughter, is a guest of the prince at Karador** while terms are discussed. Derid wants to resettle Caer Corwell as an embassy.
- **Araithe wants to understand the mortal world and has given his subjects permission to venture beyond Sarifal.**
- **Karador:** about 3,000 inhabitants, mostly fey. Very few non-fey may enter, but that restriction is loosening as the court recognizes it must engage allies in the Moonshaes and beyond.
- **The Sarifal Faithful:** an organization Araithe created so Sarifal can engage the outside world, overseen by his niece **Lady Siofra** (fond of mortals, an inconsistent steward). **Its upper ranks are mostly Llewyrr elves who act as Sarifal's emissaries.**
- The fey visit Branwyn Moonsinger, an ancient druid at a moonwell on Myrloch's shore, with gifts and questions about Gwynneth's history before their arrival.

### The Earthmother
- The spirit of nature in the Moonshaes. Neutral; her strength comes from the Balance between extremes. Her enemies are those who destroy life carelessly and those who would tame the land.
- No set form; she *is* the land. Symbols: the swallow (messenger), the oak (strength), the pine (the Balance), and above all **the moon and the sun together**.
- Worshiped in druid groves. **Her moonwells are "her windows onto the world"**; their powers are not common knowledge. Full-moon nights are high holy times; solstices and equinoxes are celebrated.
- Her three children: the Leviathan, Kamerynn the Unicorn, and the Pack.
- Her antithesis is Kazgoroth the Beast, which corrupts moonwells.
- Debated nature: Chauntea's clerics call her an aspect of Chauntea; the Ffolk druids call her a primal spirit of the land.
- Other Moonshae faiths: Chauntea, Helm (popular for opposing Talos and Malar), Milil; Valkur among Northlanders, with fearful offerings to Umberlee. **Talos has long targeted the Moonshaes and House Kendrick**; sages wonder whether his hand is at work again.

### Trade and the sea
- The **Moonshae Trade League** (based on Snowdown, Amnian roots, now with Waterdeep, Luskan and Baldur's Gate money) builds trade ships that carry goods to and from the Sword Coast, Chult and the other islands.
- Reavers from the Korinn Archipelago prey on Sword Coast shipping lanes, especially between Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate.

### Where this guide differs from the older notes below
- Llewyrr numbers are rising, not shrinking.
- Amn still holds Snowdown (the wiki says Amn was expelled in the early 1480s).
- Ordalf reads as distant and prophecy-bound rather than simply cruel, and Sarifal's isolation is softening.

## The Moonshae Isles in brief

- An archipelago in the Sea of Swords, west of the Sword Coast. Six major islands: Alaron, Gwynneth, Moray, Norland, Oman, Snowdown.
- **Peoples:** the Ffolk (southern farmers and traders who revere the Earthmother through druids and moonwells), Northlanders (northern warriors and raiders who worship Tempus, Auril, Umberlee, and increasingly Talos and Valkur), the Llewyrr elves, and the fey of Sarifal. Dwarves and halflings hold scattered communities.
- One of the greatest centers of druidic power in Faerûn.
- **Politics by the 1480s DR:** House Kendrick's High King Derid rules mainly Alaron. Gwynneth is under fey control as Sarifal.
- **Trade with the mainland:** Waterdeep, Calimshan, Mintarn, Amn, Tethyr. The isles export timber and metal goods.
- **15th century:** The barrier between the isles and the Feywild weakened badly, bringing a flood of fey. After the Second Sundering, fey reportedly began vanishing from the isles for unexplained reasons.

## Gwynneth and Sarifal

- Gwynneth is the largest of the southern isles. Its heart is the **Myrloch Vale** and **Lake Myrloch**.
- **1375 DR:** The fey city of **Karador** rose out of Lake Myrloch from the Feywild. Its ruler proclaimed herself queen of Gwynneth, and the human kingdom of Corwell fell into ruin.
- **Sarifal** is the fey and elven kingdom that now covers Gwynneth. Visitors describe it as breathtakingly beautiful and unsettling: sights and sounds seem to play tricks on perception.
- **By 1412 DR**, all non-fey had been expelled from the island. Sarifal has kept strict isolation since, refusing even the High King's requests for an embassy as late as 1489 DR.
- **Karador:** the capital, in the middle of Lake Myrloch. A powerful mythal can shift the city between the world and the Feywild. Inhabitants are mostly Llewyrr elves, wood elves, gnomes, and other fey, with almost no humans. Ancient leShay fey serve as arcanists more powerful than elven mages.
- **The symbol of the royal house:** a climbing rose, the Rose of Sarifal.

### The court
- **High Lady Ordalf** (Queen leShay): an ancient, nearly immortal fey ruler of more than 11,000 years. She appears as a dark-haired, golden-eyed eladrin maiden and rules from a crystal throne. Arrogant and cruel, she views all mortal races, elves included, as lesser. She enforces isolation; many subjects dislike it, but few dare defy her. She keeps spies in the Kendrick courts.
- **Prince Araithe:** her son. Since the Spellplague weakened her, he handles most day-to-day government, which has made the court unstable.
- **Amaranth:** Ordalf's half-sister and rival claimant to the throne.
- Dissidents and rival archfey challenge Ordalf, but she remains the most influential ruler in the Moonshae Isles into the late 1400s DR.
- **Isolation has cracks:** some eladrin nobles (one named example: Hadarai El'or'riandir) pursue trade with the outside world despite the isolation policy, and Ordalf keeps spies in the Kendrick courts. Sarifal is officially closed but not deaf to what happens beyond Gwynneth.

## The Llewyrr and Synnoria

- The **Llewyrr** are a Moonshae people of high elves, likely descended from survivors of an elven ship wrecked on the way to Evermeet.
- Around −9800 DR they founded **Synnoria**, a hidden kingdom in the mountains bordering the Myrloch Vale, named for the queen who led them over the sea.
- **Hidden by design:** A powerful mass illusion hides Synnoria from the air. Its narrow, treacherous mountain passes confuse anyone without a guide.
- **Chrysalis:** their capital, a dazzling settlement of glass, crystal, and silver.
- **Culture:** vegetarian; trout are sacred. Low birth rates and losses in war, especially against firbolgs, have shrunk their numbers. Women took over duties traditionally held by men, including defense.
- **The Sisters of Synnoria:** warrior women on white chargers with silver lances. In 1345 DR they rode in service of Prince Tristan Kendrick.
- **Notable places:**
  - **Mirror Lake:** an enchanted lake said to answer questions.
  - **The Grove of Meditation:** enhances elven magical learning.
  - **The singing waterfalls:** fed by glacial runoff, with an entrancing sound.
- **History:** Synnoria historically lived in peace with dwarves but withdrew into isolation as humans colonized the isles. In 1365 DR, an attack destroyed the palace Argen-Tellirynd.
- **Under Sarifal:** The Llewyrr historically swear fealty to the High Lady of Sarifal, and Synnoria is a subkingdom of her realm.

## Faith notes from the *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* (supplied by the director)

- The Seldarine dwell in Arvandor on the plane of Arborea. Corellon Larethian leads them: god of elves, magic, poetry, rulership and warcraft.
- **Angharradh:** triune goddess of wisdom and fierce mother-protector of the elves, Corellon's consort. Her three aspects are Aerdrie Faenya (winds and weather), Hanali Celanil (love, beauty, art, enchantment), and Sehanine Moonbow (life's mysteries: mysticism, prophecy, death, dreams). In legend these are often separate goddesses.
- **Labelas Enoreth:** the philosopher god of time and history, **whose gift of trance is crucial to elven identity and survival.**
- Many elves also worship Faerûnian gods such as Mielikki, Lurue, Silvanus and Sune, and lately some have taken to Lathander.

## Faith option: Labelas Enoreth

- Elven god of time, history, and the orderly passage of ages. Title: the Lord of the Continuum. Symbol: the setting sun.
- Knowledge is among his 5th-edition cleric domains.
- Worshipers include sages, historians, philosophers, and librarians.
- **Dogma:** patience, sharing wisdom, preserving knowledge through records and teaching; opposes decay, entropy, and undeath.
- Works with Sehanine Moonbow to watch over the long lives of elves.
- His priests gather in groves **at sunset** to pray, meditate, and share knowledge, marking the passing of another day.

## Elven age (2024 rules)

- Elves reach adulthood at **100** and can live to about **750**.
