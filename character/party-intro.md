# Party Introduction

Tetia's introduction for the other players, in the same style as their roster posts. **Deliberately limited to what someone would notice on first meeting her**, plus her manner. Everything else (her parents, the court, her mission, the Faithful, her telepathy, her faith) is for the party to learn in play.

**Name:** Tetia Mercury (TEH-tee-uh)
**Race:** Elf
**Class:** Cleric (Knowledge Domain)
**Age:** Looks to be in her early twenties to mortal eyes; still young, even for an elf
**Height:** 5'10"

**Appearance:** Tall and slender, with very long, fine white-silver hair that falls nearly to the backs of her knees, pinned at the sides with small gold hairpins set with lilac flowers and pearls. Long elven ears stand out well past it. Her skin is porcelain-pale and colors at the slightest cold or embarrassment; her eyes are a soft gray-hazel under dark lashes, with brows a shade of ash-brown darker than her hair.

She wears delicate silver half plate worked with gold filigree and small blue gems over a white gown with long, wide sleeves and blue panels trimmed in gold, and a deep blue hooded cloak over it all when the weather turns. A high white collar holds a blue pendant at her throat. She carries a plain shield, and no weapon at all. The armor looks heavier on her than it should. It clinks and chimes softly with every step; she could not sneak up on anyone.

**Demeanor:** Formal, soft-spoken and unfailingly polite, often more formal than the moment calls for. Her voice is clear and melodic. She is composed and watchful, keeps a courteous distance from people she doesn't know, and asks a great many questions while answering very few. She tends to write things down in the middle of conversations.

**Notable gear:** The blue pendant at her collar, which she touches when she prays; a well-used journal and a writing kit she is rarely without.

What brings her to Leilon, she will explain in her own time.
