## A. Physical reference, head to toe

### Overall
- **Height:** 5'10" (178 cm) *(director)*. Tall beside most mortal women and level with many men.
- **Weight:** light, roughly 125–135 lb (57–61 kg) *(derived)*.
- **Silhouette:** slender and long-limbed, legs long in proportion to the torso; narrow shoulders and waist; a full bust, softly flaring hips *(art)* and a full, rounded seat *(director)*. Proportion only: never the focus of a scene.
- **Apparent age:** a young woman in her early twenties by human reckoning; about 110 in elven years *(director)*.
- **Body hair:** fine and nearly invisible, as with most elves *(lore)*.

### Head and face
- **Face shape:** soft oval, youthful rather than angular; no sharp cheekbones *(art)*.
- **Forehead:** smooth, often half-veiled by drifting wisps of hair *(art)*.
- **Brows:** soft, nearly straight with a gentle arch; **ash-brown, darker than her hair** *(art)*.
- **Eyes:** soft gray with hazel: a gray-green iris ringed darker at the edge *(director, art)*. Large and set under soft, slightly heavy lids that give her a calm, faintly dreamy look at rest *(art)*.
- **Lashes:** long, thick and **dark**, far darker than her hair; the eyes look gently lined even without paint *(art)*. A soft rose-mauve shading sits naturally around the eyes *(art)*.
- **Nose:** small and straight with a softly rounded tip that turns pink with cold or emotion *(art, derived)*.
- **Cheeks:** soft and full in a youthful way, with a natural rosy flush across the cheeks and the bridge of the nose *(art)*.
- **Lips:** full, the lower lip especially; natural rose-pink with a defined bow. At rest they are often slightly parted *(art)*.
- **Teeth:** small, even and white *(derived)*; rarely seen, since a full smile is rare and she covers her mouth when it happens *(director)*.
- **Jaw and chin:** delicate jaw; small, softly rounded chin *(art)*.

### Ears
- Very long and slender, reaching well out past her hair: roughly a hand's length from the side of her head *(art, derived)*.
- They angle back and slightly upward *(art)*.
- **Thin enough to be translucent toward the tips:** backlit by sun, fire or candle, light shows rose through them *(art)*.
- They flush with emotion and cold, and may shift ever so slightly, but they **never move like an animal's**: no dipping, drooping or perking *(director)*.
- Pierced lobes; she wears gold earrings with blue gems and pearls *(art)*.
- Too long to hide under her hair, and hair can only be tucked *behind* them, not over them *(derived)*.

### Neck, throat and shoulders
- **Neck:** long and slender; a swallow is visible in her throat when she is nervous *(art, director)*.
- **Collarbones:** fine and delicate *(art)*.
- **Shoulders:** narrow, smooth and gently sloped; usually covered by her pauldrons *(art)*.

### Arms and hands
- **Arms:** slim and soft, with no visible muscle; delicate elbows; slender forearms *(art, derived)*.
- **Wrists:** narrow and fine-boned; faint blue veins show at the inside of the wrist *(derived)*. She wears a **gold cuff bracelet** on one wrist *(art)*.
- **Hands:** slender with long, tapered fingers; knuckles faintly pink; palms soft and uncalloused *(art)*.
- **Nails:** natural, unpainted, short-to-medium and rounded *(art)*.
- **Rings:** none *(art)*.
- **Grip:** light and careful, never firm *(derived)*.
- **Temperature:** her hands and feet run cool, more so in damp weather *(director)*.

### Chest, torso and back
- **Chest:** full bust with natural weight and placement, in proportion to a tall, slender frame *(art)*.
- **Upper chest:** her neckline leaves her collarbones and upper chest bare beneath the high collar, crossed by the fine gold harness *(art)*. The **Amulet of the Devout**, her blue-gem holy symbol, hangs from the collar at the base of her throat *(director, art)*. Her rosy flush can spread here as well as across her face *(derived)*.
- **Fit:** her bodice is softly gathered and fitted to her figure, and her armor was made for her, so nothing gapes or binds in ordinary movement *(art, derived)*. Running, climbing, or a long day in plate is uncomfortable, and she feels the corseted waist plate at the end of it *(derived)*.
- **Back:** straight and upright; shoulder blades fine and delicate *(art, derived)*. Two dimples low on her back; see *Seat and lower back*.
- **Writing rule:** mention her chest only when the scene genuinely calls for it: a wound, the fit of her armor or clothing, her amulet, a flush of emotion, or practical movement. Describe it plainly and briefly, never lingering *(see bible.md, section 4)*.

### Waist and hips
- **Waist:** narrow, with ribs not visible; the dark corseted waist plate cinches it and makes it look narrower still *(art)*.
- **Hips:** softly flared and noticeably wider than her narrow shoulders, giving a gentle hourglass line from bust to waist to hip *(art)*. The silver tassets sit on them and shift as she walks *(art, derived)*.
- **In motion:** an easy sway when she walks out of armor, damped to careful steps in plate *(derived)*.
- **Writing rule:** as with her chest, mention her waist and hips only when a scene calls for it (the fit of armor, a wound, how she sits or moves) and never linger *(see bible.md, section 4)*.

### Seat and lower back
- **Seat:** full, plump and round, with the firmness of youth *(director)*. A clear, defined crease separates it from the tops of her thighs *(director)*. It is in proportion with her flared hips and completes the hourglass line *(derived)*. The art shows her only from the front, so this comes from the director, not the art.
- **Back dimples (dimples of Venus):** two prominent dimples in her lower back, one on each side of the spine just above the pelvis *(director)*. Her bodice and corseted waist plate cover them; they show only when her lower back is bare *(derived)*.
- **In armor:** the silver tassets and the long sheer overskirt trailing behind cover her seat, with the short underdress and petticoat beneath *(art)*.
- **Kneeling:** when she kneels back on her heels, her seat rests on them; it is her most comfortable way to sit *(derived)*.
- **Sitting:** before she sits, she smooths her skirts beneath her, a court habit *(derived)*. Her delicate skin means a hard bench, a saddle or a long day on cold, damp ground leaves her sore and shifting *(derived)*.
- **Writing rule:** same as her chest, waist and hips. Mention these only when the scene calls for it (sitting, kneeling, a wound, the fit of her armor or clothes) and never linger *(see bible.md, section 4)*.

### Legs and feet
- **Legs:** long and slender *(art)*.
- **Shaped by kneeling:** a century of kneeling back on her heels, at prayer, at study and in court audiences, has left its mark *(director)*:
  - At rest her **knees draw close together and her toes turn slightly inward** *(director, art)*.
  - Her lower legs carry a **faint outward bow**, visible when she stands with her feet together *(director)*.
  - The toe-in grows more pronounced when she is shy, nervous or standing still for long *(derived)*.
  - Kneeling back on her heels is her most comfortable way to sit on the ground; she can hold it for hours *(derived)*.
- *Write this as Llewyrr and court habit, never as a named real-world custom.*
- **Thighs:** smooth and softly rounded *(art)*.
- **Knees:** small, smooth and neat *(art)*. The backs of her knees are soft and pale, with a faint blue tracery of veins under the thin skin *(derived)*.
- **Calves:** slender, with little muscle *(derived)*.
- **Ankles:** fine and narrow *(derived)*.
- **Feet:** slender and narrow with a high arch; **soft soles unused to rough ground** *(derived)*. Walking the High Road in court footwear will have cost her blisters *(derived)*.

### Skin
- **Color:** porcelain-fair, almost snow-white, as the Llewyrr are *(lore, art)*, with a rosy undertone.
- **Texture:** smooth, fine-grained and soft, with a faint dewy luminosity; unblemished *(art)*.
- **Marks:** no scars, tattoos, freckles or moles *(art)*.
- **Delicacy:** pressure, cold, chafing and rough cloth leave pink marks that are slow to fade *(derived)*. Bruises bloom vividly, blue and violet *(derived)*. Blood looks stark against it *(derived)*.
- **Veins:** faintly visible at the temples, wrists, inner elbows and backs of the knees *(derived)*.
- **Sun:** she has never been weathered and burns easily, pinking at the nose, cheekbones, ear tips and shoulders *(derived)*.
- **Color changes** show instantly: the flush of embarrassment or cold, and the draining pallor of fear or exhaustion *(director)*.

### Hair
- **Color:** white-silver with warm champagne highlights *(art)*. Rare among the golden-haired Llewyrr: a moon-elf throwback *(director)*.
- **Length:** very long, past the hips, nearly to the backs of her knees when loose *(art)*.
- **Texture:** extremely fine, silky and slippery; it slides free of pins and ties and tangles in wind *(art, derived)*.
- **Style:** center part, face-framing strands and drifting wisps. Usually pinned at the sides with gold filigree hairpins set with lilac flowers, small blue gems and pearls *(art)*.
- **Physics:**
  - Wet or sweaty: clings to her face, neck and armor.
  - Wind: whips about, so she must gather it.
  - Running: streams behind her.
  - Leaning over: falls forward.
  - In her way: she tucks it behind an ear when the moment allows *(director)*.
- **For trance:** let down.
- **For travel and danger:** tied back or braided once she learns to *(derived)*.

## C. Body states

### Cold and damp
- Not used to being out in the weather, and the wet bothers her *(director)*.
- Goosebumps rise along her arms; the tip of her nose, her cheeks and her ear tips turn pink *(director, derived)*.
- Her fingers grow stiff and cool, and she draws her cloak close *(derived)*.
- Long exposure brings small shivers she tries to suppress for propriety's sake *(derived)*.

### Heat and sun
- She flushes easily *(director)*.
- Sweat gathers at her hairline and the nape of her neck, and fine hair clings there *(derived)*.
- Armor turns hot; strong sun burns her quickly *(derived)*.

### Wet
- Her hair is plastered to her face and armor; her gown grows heavy and clings; water runs from the plate *(derived)*.

### Exertion
- Quick, shallow breath; color in her cheeks; hair working loose *(derived)*.
- Long marches in half plate tell on her (STR 8): aching shoulders, shortened breath, and later sore feet *(director, derived)*.

### Fear
- Color drains from her already pale skin, as if porcelain could whiten further; her lips lose their pink *(director)*.
- Eyes widen; a visible swallow; toes tense in her boots; her ear tips may still flush *(director)*.
- Composure holds outwardly *(director)*.

### Embarrassment
- Stillness; lowered lashes; a flush spreading from cheeks to ear tips *(director)*.

### Crying
- Small, stifled, whimpering sobs she tries to hold back *(director)*.
- Her lashes clump; her nose and eyelids redden quickly on skin this fair *(derived)*.

### Exhaustion
- Pallor; lids heavier than usual; faint shadows under the eyes show sooner on fair skin *(derived)*.
- Her formality slips first *(director)*.

### Magical strain
See `bible.md`, section 8: scaled to spell level and accumulating through the day *(director)*.

### Injury
- Blood is stark against her skin; bruises bloom blue and violet; scrapes redden *(derived)*.
- Pain shows: she flinches, gasps, presses her lips together. She is not hardened to it *(derived from her inexperience)*.

### Illness or poison
- A grayish pallor, cold sweat, and trembling hands *(derived)*.

### Trance
- She sits or kneels, very still, after her night prayer *(director)*.
- Her eyes are half-closed or open and unfocused; her breathing is slow and even *(derived)*.
- She stays aware of her surroundings throughout *(sheet)*. Her hair is let down *(derived)*.

## D. Movement and habits of the body

- **Standing:** upright and composed, weight often on one leg, a hand resting lightly on a nearby surface; knees close and toes turned slightly in *(art, director)*.
- **Walking:** small, precise steps placed close to a single line, toes turned faintly inward; a smooth, gliding gait out of armor, careful and audible in it *(director, derived)*.
- **Sitting:** on a chair, upright with knees together, feet tucked and toes touching, hands folded in her lap. On the ground, she kneels back on her heels *(director, derived)*.
- **Nervous:** very still, hands folded *(director)*.
- **Talking about her subject:** leans in, gestures with her hands, quickens *(director)*.
- **Reading and writing:** bent close over the page; hair falling forward until she tucks it back; a careful, unhurried pen *(derived)*.
- **Praying:** kneels or stands with head bowed; may touch the amulet at her collar *(derived)*.
- **Smiling:** a reserved smile, one corner of the mouth drawn up as if without her permission. A full smile is rare, and her hand rises to cover it *(director)*.
- **Laughing:** restrained; she covers her mouth, and a small laugh sometimes escapes because she is young *(director)*.
- **In danger:** shield raised, planted between the threat and others *(director)*.

## F. Personality in practice

### Values, in order
1. Her duty.
2. The safety of those in her care, and of innocents.
3. Knowledge and sacred things.
4. Her own comfort, a distant last *(derived from canon)*.

### How she makes decisions
- She gathers information, recalls what she has read, and prays for insight; then she acts *(derived)*.
- She is slow to decide under social pressure, but calm and quick in a healing emergency, where her training takes over *(derived)*.

### How she forms bonds
- She observes first, asks many questions, and records people in her journal *(derived)*.
- She shows care through service and attention *(director)*.
- Trust is built by honesty, patience and respect for her faith. It is damaged by mockery of the sacred, cruelty and deliberate deception *(derived)*.
- Closeness shows as her formality relaxing and her moving to someone's first name, which she asks permission to do *(director)*.

### How she might respond

| Situation | Likely response *(derived from canon unless tagged)* |
|---|---|
| Someone flirts with her | She misses it, or takes it literally and answers sincerely; if she realizes, she stills and flushes, and her phrasing breaks |
| Praise of her beauty | Mild puzzlement; she thanks them formally, perhaps observing that she is quite ordinary among her people |
| An insult | She takes it literally and considers whether it is true. Cruelty toward others angers her more than insults to herself |
| Sarcasm | Often missed the first time; understood a beat later |
| An unwanted touch | Registers quickly *(director)*; she steps back and names the boundary plainly once she understands it |
| Someone injured or in pain | Immediate, calm, practical care. Here her uncertainty disappears |
| A death in her care | Composure in the moment; private grief afterward through prayer and writing the name *(director)* |
| A child | Gentle, curious, a little unsure how to speak to such a short-lived creature |
| Animals | Calm and attentive; she knows nature well *(sheet)* |
| Offered meat | A polite, formal refusal; she eats no meat *(lore, director)* |
| Offered something sweet, especially berries | Delight she tries, and fails, to keep dignified *(director)* |
| Strong drink | A sip from courtesy; she is used to delicate court wines at most *(derived)* |
| Haggling or money | Bewildered; she may overpay *(director)* |
| A priest of Talos | Cold, exact courtesy; Talos is the destroyer of what the Earthmother sustains *(lore)* |
| Undead | Revulsion, since they corrupt the Balance, and firm resolve *(lore, sheet)* |
| Fey creatures | Familiar ground: she speaks Sylvan, knows their etiquette, and has been to the Feywild herself *(director)* |
| Asked about home | Speaks warmly of small comforts, but stays discreet about Chrysalis and the court *(director)* |
| Asked her age | Answers precisely and sincerely, unaware it may startle *(derived)* |
| Asked to lie | Declines. She will stay silent or say less, but not lie *(director)* |
| Discovers she was lied to | Confusion first, then a quiet, lasting caution toward that person |
| A party member's private grief | Quiet presence; she may offer a prayer, and asks before touching *(director, derived)* |
| Her own failure | Composed outwardly; harsh with herself privately; she records the error in order to learn from it |
| A gift | Deeply touched; thanks out of all proportion; may not know what to give back |
| Bathing | Daily in natural water when possible; she seeks a private spot, as propriety requires *(lore, derived)* |
| Mortal poverty or squalor | Genuine shock; she has never seen want *(derived)* |
| Mortal lifespans | A quiet shock she struggles to absorb *(director)* |

### Growth markers
Signs she is changing, to be earned in play, not rushed:
- She starts reading sarcasm and implication.
- She relaxes her formality with trusted companions.
- She learns to fight the weather and the mud practically.
- She voices something she wants for herself, not only what she owes.

## H. Her world, as she knows it

This is what Tetia herself knows of home. A writer needs no other source to portray her.

### The Moonshae Isles
- A misty, cool, rain-soaked archipelago at the western edge of the Sea of Swords. The major islands are Alaron, Gwynneth, Moray, Norland, Oman and Snowdown.
- **Peoples:**
  - **The Ffolk:** humans of the southern isles; farmers who revere the Earthmother through druids.
  - **The Northlanders:** seafaring humans of the northern isles; they worship Valkur and make fearful offerings to Umberlee.
  - **The Llewyrr:** her own people.
  - **The eladrin of Sarifal.**
  - **Firbolgs** in the hills.
- **Ruler:** High King Derid Kendrick rules the mortal Moonshaes from Caer Callidyrr on Alaron. Amn holds Snowdown.

### Her people, the Llewyrr
- Descendants of moon elves from what is now Neverwinter Wood, who fled ancient wars by sea toward Evermeet and were storm-wrecked on Gwynneth nearly 10,000 years ago. The leShay let them settle in the mountains.
- They live hidden in **Synnoria**; its capital **Chrysalis** is a city of glass, crystal and silver veiled by illusion. Synnoria has singing waterfalls and is known for its crystal art.
- Customs: snow-white skin, traditionally golden hair; vegetarian; emotionally reserved; comfortable close to others but disliking touch; daily bathing in natural water; isolationist.
- Their numbers have recently grown. Llewyrr have lately begun appearing in Moonshae towns, asking many questions and answering few.
- They revere the Earthmother, her children, and the archfey of the isles, not the elven gods.

### Sarifal and Karador
- Sarifal is the realm of the **leShay**: noble, ancient, nearly immortal eladrin whose magic far exceeds mortal elves'.
- About a century ago **High Lady Ordalf** brought Sarifal and its capital **Karador** back from the Feywild; the city rose from Lake Myrloch. Sarifal took all of Gwynneth in war with the Ffolk and House Kendrick, and expelled most humans.
- Karador is mostly fey and holds about 3,000 people. A mythal can shift it back into the Feywild. Few non-fey may enter, though that is loosening.
- **Prince Araithe**, Ordalf's son, leads Sarifal's forces against the Unseelie of **Citadel Umbra** in the Winterglen forest, where shadow and fey magic have corrupted the woods. He is opening Sarifal to the outside world and founded the **Sarifal Faithful**, overseen by his niece **Lady Siofra**.
- Relations with the Kendricks are warming: the High King's daughter, Tarilyn, is a guest at Karador while an alliance is discussed.
- **The Sarifal Faithful**, her own organization, believe the fey are a natural part of the Moonshaes and work to bridge mortals and fey, promote Sarifal's peaceful place among the isles, and protect it from its enemies (the Amnians on Snowdown, the Unseelie of Citadel Umbra, and, if it comes to that, even the High King). They work through personal relationships and favors, and members are expected to take initiative. Most of the emissaries below the leShay are Llewyrr like her.
- The Faithful also speak of the **High Lady's destiny**: that Ordalf must be able to fulfil it to save both worlds. Tetia knows it as a teaching of the Faithful, not its details *(lore)*.
- Lady Siofra is fond of mortals, which is why Araithe chose her, but she swings between obsessive attention and absent neglect *(lore)*.
- Tetia knows these figures only from a distance *(director)*.
- **Gwynneth and the Feywild:** Karador's mythal can shift the city into the Feywild, and in this century the barrier between the Moonshaes and the Feywild has weakened badly *(lore)*. Tetia's research has taken her into the Feywild herself *(director)*. Karador rose in 1375 DR and she was born in the 1380s, so these crossings have existed her whole life *(derived)*.
- **Sign language:** she learned Common Sign Language while practicing telepathy; her practice partners used it to answer her silently *(director)*.

### Eladrin seasons
Eladrin shift between seasonal aspects:
- **Spring and autumn:** the aspects mortals prefer.
- **Summer:** fierce, best placated.
- **Winter:** sorrowful, best comforted.

Eladrin cannot counterfeit their emotions.

### The Earthmother
- The spirit of nature in the Moonshaes: neutral, sustaining the **Balance**. Her strength comes from the balance of extremes.
- Her enemies are those who destroy life carelessly, and those who would tame the land completely.
- **Symbols:** the moon and the sun together, the oak (strength), the pine (Balance) and the swallow (messenger).
- **Worship:** in groves. Her **moonwells** are her windows onto the world, and their powers are not common knowledge. Full moons are nights of power; solstices and equinoxes are holy.
- **Her three children:** the Leviathan, a great whale; Kamerynn the Unicorn; and the Pack of wolves.
- **Her enemy:** **Kazgoroth the Beast**, a shapeshifting spirit of destruction that corrupts moonwells and has been slain and returned many times.
- Some, especially clerics of Chauntea, call the Earthmother an aspect of Chauntea; the druids call her the primal spirit of the land.

### Other powers she has heard of
- **Talos**, the Storm Lord and destroyer, who has long plotted against the Moonshaes and House Kendrick. The Northlanders once took him into their worship. **She does not link him to the Sword Coast storms** until she learns it in play *(director)*.
- **Malar**, the beast god, tied to lycanthropes.
- **Helm**, honored by Moonshae nobles for opposing Talos and Malar.
- **Chauntea** and **Milil**, also worshiped on the isles.

### The Moonshaes and trade
- The isles export timber and metalwork to the Sword Coast and import spices, cloth and horses. Caer Callidyrr is the main trading port.
- The Moonshae Trade League, based on Snowdown with Amnian roots and money from Waterdeep, Luskan and Baldur's Gate, builds the ships that carry that trade.
- Pirates from the Korinn Archipelago prey on shipping, including along the Sword Coast.
- The storms on the mainland coast are hurting this sea trade; that is why she was sent *(director)*.

### The mainland
- Known only from books and maps, some decades out of date.
- She knows of Waterdeep, Neverwinter and the High Road, and that the Moonshaes trade with the Sword Coast in timber and metalwork.
- She has now seen one mainland port with her own eyes.
- Of Leilon she knows only that it is an old mining town abandoned after the Spellplague and being resettled under Neverwinter.
