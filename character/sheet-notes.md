## Features that shape how she acts

### Elf
- **Trance:** She does not sleep. She finishes a Long Rest in 4 hours of trancelike meditation and stays conscious throughout. Magic cannot put her to sleep.
- **Fey Ancestry:** Hard to charm.
- **Keen Senses:** Source of one of her Insight, Perception, or Survival proficiencies.
- **High Elf lineage spells (Wisdom):** Mind Sliver cantrip (swapped in from the lineage default; can change on a Long Rest), Detect Magic, and Misty Step, each castable once per Long Rest without a slot.

### Cleric
- **Divine Order — Thaumaturge:** One extra cantrip; adds Wisdom to Arcana and Religion checks. She is a scholar-priest, not a front-line protector.
- **Channel Divinity (3 per Long Rest, one regained on a Short Rest):** Divine Spark, Turn Undead (with Sear Undead: radiant damage to undead that fail), and Mind Magic.
- **Blessed Strikes — Potent Spellcasting:** +4 to damage with Cleric cantrips.

### Knowledge Domain
- **Blessings of Knowledge:** Expertise in Arcana and Nature, plus one artisan's tool.
- **Mind Magic:** Spends Channel Divinity to cast a prepared Knowledge Domain divination without a slot or material components.
- **Unfettered Mind (level 6):** Telepathy 60 ft., up to 4 creatures at once; Intelligence save proficiency.
- **Always-prepared domain spells:** Command, Comprehend Languages, Detect Magic, Detect Thoughts, Identify, Mind Spike; Dispel Magic, Nondetection, Tongues. (Arcane Eye, Banishment, and Confusion are also listed as always prepared on the export.)

### Feats
- **Magic Initiate (Wizard):** Prestidigitation, Mending; Shield once per Long Rest without a slot.
- **War Caster:** Advantage on Concentration saves; somatic components with hands full; can cast a single-target spell instead of an opportunity attack.
- **Arcane Omens:** Guidance cantrip. **Helpful Premonition:** 3 per Long Rest, as a reaction when she or a creature within 30 ft. fails a save, adds 1d4 to the roll.
- **Circle Magic Casting** (*Heroes of Faerûn*): Can lead group spellcasting with other casters lending their magic.
